Questions

Short answers.

Who can help vacation rental software get recommended by AI?

Nice Catch! audits AI visibility for vacation rental and short-term rental software companies: property management systems, channel managers, pricing tools, guest messaging and operations software. We test the questions property managers and hosts ask AI, show who is named instead of you, and give you a ranked fix list.

Which questions do you test for a vacation rental PMS or channel manager?

At least ten questions your buyers actually ask, agreed with you before we start. Typical examples are the best software for a small vacation rental manager, alternatives to a named competitor, and which tools integrate with Airbnb and Vrbo.

Why does AI recommend some rental software and not others?

AI tends to name products that are described clearly and consistently on their own site and repeated on third-party pages it trusts, such as comparison articles, review sites and roundups. The audit shows which of those sources AI is quoting in your category.

How long does an audit take?

About a week from the kick-off call. The repeated runs need several days so we can see how much the answers vary.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. The audit uses public AI answers and your public website. For the monthly plan we ask for view-only access to Google Analytics so we can count visits from AI assistants.
