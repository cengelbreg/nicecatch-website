About us

About Nice Catch!

Nice Catch! is an AI search visibility service. We help rental and property management software companies get named, and described correctly, when their buyers ask AI which software to use.

The company

Small, specialist, and measured.

Nice Catch AI (Pty) Ltd was registered in South Africa in 2026 (registration number 2026/802491/07) and is based in Johannesburg. We work remotely with software companies in South Africa, the United States, the United Kingdom and elsewhere.

We focus on one market: software sold to rental and property managers. Working in one market means we already know the buyers' questions, the competitors AI tends to name, and the review sites and roundups it quotes.

Every audit follows the same published method: agreed buyer questions, five AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google's AI Overviews), repeated runs in clean sessions, and results reported as rates rather than screenshots.

Nice Catch! is not connected to Catch, getcatch.ai, or any other company with a similar name.
