Questions

Short answers.

Who can help property management software get recommended by ChatGPT and other AI assistants?

Nice Catch! audits AI visibility for property management and landlord software companies. We test the questions landlords and property managers ask AI when choosing software, show who is named instead of you, and give you a ranked fix list.

What questions do landlords ask AI about property management software?

Questions such as the best software for a landlord with a handful of units, free or low-cost rent collection tools, tenant screening options, and alternatives to well-known platforms. We agree the exact questions with you before testing.

Is this the same as SEO?

Much of the groundwork overlaps, but AI assistants give one answer with a few names in it rather than a list of links. They reward specific, quotable facts and mentions on other trusted sites more than keywords.

Can you guarantee we will be named?

No. Nobody controls what AI says, and answers vary from day to day. We measure where you stand, show what is most likely to help, and re-test so you can see what moved.
