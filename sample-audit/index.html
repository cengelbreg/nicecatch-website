Sample audit · client details removed

What an audit looks like.

This is a real audit we ran in October 2026, shared with the client's permission. We removed the company's name, its towns and its rivals' names. Everything else, including the numbers, is as delivered.

1. Summary

Named in 24 of 240 answers (10%).

The client is a home-care and rental-inspection company. We asked ten questions an absentee homeowner or a letting agent would ask, in five AI assistants, five times each over six days.

  • Where it wins: in its main market, one assistant (Perplexity) named it in 16 of 20 answers, often first or as the only independent option.
  • Where it's missing: in its secondary market it was named in 8 of 144 answers. Gemini, Google's AI answers and Claude never recommended it.
  • The biggest gap: two assistants still described the business as it was before it moved its main focus, so they sent buyers to the wrong area.

3 & 4. Who AI names instead, and why

Rivals win where they publish what AI can quote.

In the secondary market, AI named established local rivals instead. Several publish monthly prices and a clear list of the towns they cover. AI quotes those almost word for word; it had nothing equivalent from the client.

In the main market, nobody was established, so AI picked the client. That's a position worth locking in before someone else takes it.

5. Technical and facts check

AI could read the site. It just read two different stories.

All AI crawlers were allowed. The problem was the content: half the assistants described the business by its new main market, half by its old one. Google's AI answers also had no Google Business Profile to draw on.

6. Fix list, in order

Five fixes, ranked by impact and effort.

  1. One clear description everywhere

    Homepage, About page and site settings all say who you are, your main areas and your secondary areas, with towns named. Fixes the out-of-date description in Gemini and Google.

  2. One page per service and area

    Separate pages for each service in each market, so specific questions are answered from specific pages.

  3. Plans and prices in plain text

    What's included in each visit and the report, so AI has something to quote where rivals' prices are quoted today.

  4. Google Business Profile and reviews

    Google's AI answers lean heavily on them; without one, the business doesn't appear at all.

  5. Two or three outside listings

    Credible local and industry directories that AI already cites for rivals.

7. Method

How these numbers were made.

Ten questions agreed with the client, never naming the client. Five runs per question on each assistant, morning, midday, afternoon and evening, over six days (7–12 October 2026), in temporary or logged-out sessions where the assistant allows it. Claude was checked in four of the five rounds, on our own account. AI answers vary from run to run, so results are rates, not guarantees.

The client made the changes in late October. The re-test, with the same questions and method, runs four weeks later.
